# AD-003 — Adult-oriented developmental aims

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decision owner:** Project owner  
**Scope:** L2 — Adult Readiness & Developmental Progression  
**Date:** 2026-10-13

## Context

Raising Humans recognizes childhood as a life stage with intrinsic value. However, the repository's deliberate educational focus is helping parents prepare their children for adulthood. Earlier discussion considered both adult capabilities and present developmental needs; the project owner clarified that adult preparation is the primary curriculum-design objective.

## Decision

> Raising Humans adopts preparation for adulthood as its primary educational objective.
>
> L2 defines the capabilities required to navigate adult life meaningfully, responsibly and autonomously, then maps their development backward into childhood through age-informed, capability-sensitive progressions.
>
> Childhood is recognized as intrinsically valuable, but comprehensive childhood flourishing is not the primary scope of the educational framework.
>
> Educational recommendations must respect children's present dignity, rights, well-being and developmental needs. Preparation for adulthood does not justify sacrificing childhood well-being for hypothetical future benefits.

## Rationale

This makes the project's educational purpose explicit and provides a principled starting point for defining a taxonomy of adult capabilities. Mapping those capabilities backward makes it possible to formulate developmentally appropriate aims without assuming children are miniature adults or treating chronological age as the only determinant of readiness.

Adult readiness must not be equated with total self-sufficiency: sound judgment, cooperation, seeking help, healthy relationships and appropriate interdependence are part of capable adulthood.

## Alternatives considered

- **Childhood flourishing as the primary objective:** Recognized as important, but broader than this repository's chosen educational mission.
- **Equal emphasis on present childhood and future adult aims:** Philosophically defensible, but less clear as a curriculum-design priority.
- **Fixed age-based milestones:** Rejected as too rigid; age can inform, but cannot alone determine appropriate expectations.

## Consequences

- L2 is named **Adult Readiness & Developmental Progression**.
- Curriculum design begins by identifying adult capabilities, then derives developmental progressions and contextual aims.
- L3–L5 content should trace its rationale to relevant L2 aims, while respecting L1 ethical commitments.
- The future capability taxonomy should distinguish durable human capabilities from contingent contemporary social or economic expectations.
- Enjoyment, play, relationships and present well-being retain intrinsic value; they do not need to be justified only by future usefulness.

## Review

Revisit if the project's mission or evidence about developmental appropriateness materially changes. No scheduled review date has been set.
