# Education — WHAT and HOW

This layer owns educational choices and content independently of technical representation.

## Planned foundation documents
- `CONTENT_PRINCIPLES.md`: criteria governing inclusion, values, proportionality and child-centered safeguards
- `DEVELOPMENTAL_MODEL.md`: age guidance, readiness and individual variation
- `PEDAGOGICAL_APPROACH.md`: modeling, practice, play and reflection
- `EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`: evidence, uncertainty and review
- `ASSESSMENT_PHILOSOPHY.md`: observation without child ranking
