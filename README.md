# Raising Humans

An open, evidence-informed framework helping parents nurture capable, compassionate, autonomous and fulfilled humans.

> **Status:** Architecture scaffold. Educational foundations and curriculum content are pending review.

## Structure

- [Foundation](01_foundation/README.md) — **WHY**: purpose, human flourishing, ethics and boundaries.
- [Education](02_education/README.md) — **WHAT & HOW**: content principles, developmental model, domains, competencies, activities, parenting and evidence.
- [Framework](03_framework/README.md) — **SUPPORTING SYSTEM**: design, schemas, templates, tools and decisions.
- [Governance](04_governance/README.md) — **CHANGE CONTROL**: contributions, editorial policy and reviews.

## Accepted direction

- Universal core with locally adaptable extensions.
- Pluralist ethical framework; families may draw on their own moral, cultural and spiritual traditions while respecting children's dignity and rights.
- Parents are the primary audience.
- Purpose, educational content and technical framework are distinct.
- Educational choices are governed by explicit content principles.

See the [architecture](03_framework/ARCHITECTURE.md) and [decision register](03_framework/decisions/README.md).

The project is not a child optimization system. No identifiable child data belongs in this public repository. Content must be usable without software.

**This first commit series establishes structure only.** Proposed educational claims, assessment methods and licenses are not yet endorsed.
